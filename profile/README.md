## What it does

- **Real native apps.** SwiftUI for iPhone, Kotlin for Android, plus web, macOS and the backend (Python, Go or Java with MySQL / MongoDB). Not a web page wrapped in an app.
- **Scan to install.** Every build lands in the chat as a QR code. Point your phone at it and the app is on your phone.
- **Co-vibe.** Invite your boss, your PM or your testers into the same chat. They test, report and @mention the AI, and the fix happens in the same thread.
- **Deploy to your own server.** We open a machine in *your* AWS or Alibaba Cloud account, give it your domain and an HTTPS certificate, or you attach a server you already have.
- **Your Apple developer account.** Apps get your bundle prefix and your team's signature, and iOS builds go straight to your TestFlight.
- **Your GitHub.** Connect an empty repo and every change the AI makes is pushed to it automatically.

## You own everything

The code, the builds (APK, AAB, IPA), the store listings under your own developer accounts, the servers in your own cloud account. Export your project any time, and it keeps working without us.
